Epic of Gilgamesh

The Epic of Gilgamesh is a series of clay tablets written in the Sumerian Language. However, Akkadian translations were written by Sîn-lēqi-unninni (𒁹𒀭𒌍𒋾𒀀𒅆). The epic is about Gilgamesh/Bilgamesh, the tyranical king of Uruk (𒀕𒆠). He ruled Uruk with an iron fist. The gods created Enkidu (𒂗𒆠𒄭), a wild creature made to humble Gilgamesh. After becoming civilised through sexual initiation with Shamhat, Enkidu headed to Uruk to find Gilgamesh. After losing the battle against Gilgamesh, the two became best friends and take a 6-day journey to the Cedar Forest to slay it's guardian, Humbaba. Together, Gilgamesh and Enkidu slay Humbaba and cut down the sacred cedar. Ishtar, the goddess of love, war, and sex sent the Bull of Heaven to kill Gilgamesh and Enkidu. However, the Bull of Heaven was slain by the duo, and Ishtar was insulted. This led to Enkidu being punished by the gods, recieving a fatal illness. After Enkidu's death, Gilgamesh took a long, dangerous journey to seek the secret to eternal life. He meets Utnapishtim (𒌓𒍣) and his wife, the only humans to survive the flood triggered by the gods. Gilgamesh learns from Utnapishtim that when the gods made man, they let death be his share, and life withheld in their own hands (basically humans will never reach eternal life).
